# Synapsor

> Synapsor (Wuxi, China) develops AI-powered, medical-grade smart wearable devices, best known for the Ottai continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) system — a waterproof, calibration-free wearable sensor with a companion mobile app and AI assistant for 24/7 glucose tracking and diabetes management. Backed by Qiming Venture Partners, Huatai Zijin Investment, Wuxi Industry Development Group, YuanBio Venture Capital, and Shunwei Capital. Synapsor publishes no public developer API, developer portal, or machine-readable API specifications; api.synapsor.com serves a marketing page, not an API.
